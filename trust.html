Trust & safety

Travel with confidence.

A good stay starts with clear information and fair rules for everyone involved.

  • Clear prices

    The full cost, with nights, cleaning, service fee and taxes, is shown before you pay. No surprises at checkout.

  • Honest reviews

    Only guests who have finished a stay can review it, once per stay. Hosts can reply publicly, and review their guests too.

  • Protected accounts

    Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. Roles are enforced by database rules, so a guest can't open host or admin tools.

  • Fair moderation

    Anyone can report a listing or a review. Reports go to an admin console, where they can be resolved, dismissed or acted on.

Seen something wrong?

Use the Report button on a stay page or next to a review, choose a reason and send it. Reports are reviewed by the StayNest team.

For questions about a booking, start with the Help centre, or write to the host from your Messages.

Your information

We ask only for what a booking needs: your name, email and a way to pay. Card details are checked in your browser and are never stored.

StayNest is a portfolio project, so payments are simulated and bookings, saved stays and messages stay in your own browser.
