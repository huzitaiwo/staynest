Notes for the unhurried.

Short field notes on choosing well, packing light and staying put a little longer.

  • Slow travelSeptember 2026 Why one place for a week beats three for a weekend The best parts of a trip are rarely on the itinerary. Read the note

    Moving less leaves room for the things you can't plan: the bakery you start to recognise, the walk you repeat because it was good the first time. A single base turns a trip into a short life in another place.

    Pick somewhere with a kitchen, a table that suits you and a neighbourhood worth walking. Then let the days be uneven.

  • ChoosingAugust 2026 Five things to read before you book Photos tell you how a place looks. These tell you how it lives. Read the note

    Start with the area description and the house rules, then the most recent reviews. Check the sleeping arrangements against who is actually coming, and look at what's included in the final price.

    Finally, read how the host answers questions. A quick, clear reply before you book is usually a good sign of the stay itself.

  • PlacesJuly 2026 Four cities, four different kinds of quiet Lagos, Marrakech, Kyoto and Copenhagen, and what each does best. Read the note

    Lagos is quiet in the early morning, before the day finds its pace. Marrakech goes quiet behind its walls, in courtyards you'd never guess from the street.

    Kyoto's quiet is made of timber and gravel, and Copenhagen's of bicycles and long evening light. Each rewards staying a little longer than planned.

  • PracticalJune 2026 Pack for the stay, not the trip A shorter list makes every arrival easier. Read the note

    If your stay has a washing machine, you need half of what you think you do. Bring one outfit for the occasion you're nervous about, and leave the rest of the "just in case" at home.

    Use the space you save for something local: a bag from the market, a book, a bottle you'll finish before you leave.
