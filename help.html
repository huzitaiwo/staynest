How can we help?

Quick answers about booking, paying, hosting and your account.

StayNest is a portfolio project. Bookings, messages and payments are simulated and saved only in your browser.

How do I book a stay?

Open a stay, choose your dates and guests, then select Reserve. Add your details, pay, and you'll get a reservation reference. Every booking lives under Trips.

Can I cancel or change a trip?

You can cancel an upcoming trip from Trips. Changing dates isn't supported yet, so cancel and book again. In this demo no refund is processed.

How long can I stay?

A single booking can be up to 30 nights. Check-in can't be in the past, and dates a host has blocked or that are already booked can't be selected.

How does payment work here?

Payment is simulated and no real money moves. Use card 4242 4242 4242 4242 with any future expiry and any 3-digit code to succeed, or 4000 0000 0000 0002 to see a declined payment. Card details are checked in your browser and never stored.

What's included in the price?

Your total is the nightly rate multiplied by your nights, plus a cleaning fee, a service fee and taxes. The full breakdown is shown before you pay.

How do I become a host?

Sign in, choose Become a host, then create a listing. It takes nine short steps, and you can save a draft and finish later. Nothing goes live until you publish.

How do I block dates?

Open Hosting → Calendar, pick a listing and select any available date to block it. You can also block a whole range at once. Booked nights can't be changed from there.

How are my earnings counted?

Hosting → Earnings adds up the nightly rate and cleaning fee for each booking, in the month of check-in. Completed stays and upcoming ones are shown separately.

Who can leave a review?

Guests can review a stay after check-out from Trips → Past. Hosts can review their guests from Hosting → Reservations. Each side can leave one review per stay, and hosts can reply publicly to guest reviews.

How do I report a listing or review?

Use the Report button on the stay page or beside a review, choose a reason, and send it. Reports go to our admin console for review.

How do I create an account?

Sign up with your name, email and a password. New accounts start as guests, and you can become a host at any time.

Where is my information kept?

Account sign-in is handled by Firebase. Bookings, saved stays and messages are kept in your own browser for now, so clearing your site data removes them.
